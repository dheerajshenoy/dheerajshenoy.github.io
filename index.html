I'm Dheeraj, a Cosmology PhD student at Instituto de Fisica de Cantabria (IFCA), Santander, Cantabria, Spain. My research sits at the intersection of the early universe and modern computation — I work with CMB data and apply machine learning techniques to extract cosmological signals from noise.


Outside research, I build open-source tools to scratch my own itches and sharpen my craft. I'm drawn to software that is fast, minimal, and respects the user's time.

Research Interests

  • Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) — analysing the oldest light in the universe to constrain cosmological parameters.
  • Machine Learning in Astrophysics — applying deep learning to separate cosmological signals from foreground noise.
  • Data Analysis & Simulation — building pipelines that turn raw observational data into science-ready results.

Side Projects
